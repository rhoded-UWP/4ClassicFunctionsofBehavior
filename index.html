Quick reference: the four functions

Attention

“I only count when someone is noticing me.”

When corrected: stops, then starts again, often in a new form.

Teacher feels: annoyed.

Power

“I only count when I'm the one in control.”

When corrected: escalates, or complies halfway, on their own terms.

Teacher feels: challenged, angry.

Revenge

“I've been hurt, so I'll hurt back.”

When corrected: gets more personal and more hurtful.

Teacher feels: hurt.

Cover Up

“Better to look bad than to look like I can't.”

When corrected: withdraws, shuts down, does less.

Teacher feels: helpless.
